# Experimental pages in the web dashboard

## Context

The History page and the project-level records (Project records, now the "Not attached to a feature" section of the Outside features page, see [007](007-outside-features-page.md)) of the web dashboard are still evolving. They should not show up for every user of `features serve` / `features build` yet, but must stay available to try them, in the online demo and in the CI.

## Requirements

- History (`/history`) and the "Not attached to a feature" section of Outside features (`/outside-features`) are experimental.
- They are hidden by default: no History sidebar entry and `/history` redirects to the features page; the records section is hidden and not counted in the Outside features sidebar badge.
- The "Not merged across repositories" section (aggregated reports) is not experimental: Outside features stays available for aggregated reports. For a single-repository report without experimental features, Outside features has nothing to show: no sidebar entry, and `/outside-features` (and the former `/records`) redirect to the features page.
- `features serve --experimental` and `features build --experimental` enable them. The flag is written as `"experimental": true|false` in `metadata.json` (served by `serve`, generated by `build`).
- When developing the web app, `VITE_EXPERIMENTAL_FEATURES=true` enables them without `metadata.json`.
- When enabled, they are marked as experimental: a flask badge on the History sidebar entry (and on Outside features when it only holds project records) and an "Experimental" badge in the History header and the records section title.
- The CI enables them by default: the `metadata.json` of the end-to-end tests (`validate.yml`) and of the online demo (`deploy-demo.yml`) has `"experimental": true`.
- The deprecated flag forms (`features PATH --serve`, `--build`) keep the experimental pages disabled.

## Out of scope

- The specs & decisions tab of a feature (only the project-level records are experimental).
- `features aggregate` builds: no `--experimental` flag.
- A runtime toggle in the UI.

## Plan

1. CLI: `--experimental` on `ServerArgs` and `BuildArgs`, carried by `ServerConfig::with_experimental` and `BuildConfig::with_experimental` into `metadata.json`.
2. Web: `experimental` in `Metadata`, `useExperimentalFeatures` hook, `ExperimentalBadge` and `ExperimentalRoute` components; gate the History sidebar entry and route, and the records section of Outside features; badge them.
3. CI: add `"experimental": true` to the hand-written `metadata.json` of `validate.yml` (e2e) and `deploy-demo.yml`.
4. Docs: `docs/cli.md` option table, `projects/web/README.md`.
